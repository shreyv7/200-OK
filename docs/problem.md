How might we design an agentic AI curator that deeply understands an individual’s aspirations,  
habits, and evolving identity—and continuously curates the most relevant media, knowledge,  
and experiences to help them become the self they imagine?  
Individuals seeking personal growth also face a second challenge: curating the right resources  
at the right time in their journey. The goal is to transform digital media consumption from  
passive scrolling into purposeful growth, helping individuals discover the right ideas, stories,  
tools, and mentors at the right moment in their journey.  
Today’s algorithms optimize for attention. This challenge asks you to build one that optimizes  
for human potential.
